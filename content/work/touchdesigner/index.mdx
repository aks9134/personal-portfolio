Over six weeks in June and July 2026 I built 17 small projects in TouchDesigner, almost all of them real-time webcam effects driven by hand tracking. The tracking comes from the MediaPipe TouchDesigner components. What I built is what sits on top: windows your fingers define, trails that reveal a filtered copy of the image, lightning between fingertips.

The energy effect went through 25 saved versions, more than any other project. These five made it to video, and the clips here are silent.

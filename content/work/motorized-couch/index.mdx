The summer before college I turned a loveseat into something you could drive. The frame and chassis are scrap steel, welded together, and a rear module welded on behind the backrest carries a 470cc engine and the rear wheels we pulled from a go-kart.

<Strip items={[{ name: "couch-front", label: "Front" }, { name: "couch-side", label: "Side" }]} caption="From the front, you can see the couch sitting on its welded frame. From the side, you can see the engine module and a rear wheel behind the backrest." />

The front wheels hang off a central axle, and a rod rises from it between the cushions and turns in a slot in the frame, so you steer by twisting a post beside you. The brake and throttle run from the engine up through the same rod.

Only these two photos survive.

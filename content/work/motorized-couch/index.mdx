The summer before college I turned a loveseat into something you could drive. The frame and chassis are scrap steel, welded together, and a rear module welded on behind the backrest carries a 470cc engine and the rear wheels we pulled from a go-kart.

<Figure name="couch-front" caption="From the front, on the welded frame." narrow />

Steering is the unusual part. The front wheels hang off a central axle, and a rod rises from it between the cushions and turns in a slot in the frame, so you steer by twisting a post beside you. The brake and throttle run from the engine up through the same rod.

Only these two photos survive.

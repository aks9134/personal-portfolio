# Home page brief

Scope: `/` (home). Mode: Experience. Visitor: hardware-startup hiring manager, 30 seconds, laptop. Action: email Allen or open LinkedIn. Proof: capstone exploded view with parts legend, award, open-loop result; four case studies led by their images. Constraints: PRODUCT.md, employer gate, voice framework.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is Allen's shop traveler: his work routed as real hardware, each project carrying its true status as an ink stamp. It refuses the category default of a dark hero over a grid of same-size thumbnails.

OWN-WORLD: Goldenrod traveler card stock as the entire ground, black print ink, one blue stamp ink used only for status, the award and the active part. Archivo throughout, wide bold for display and stamps. 1.5px ink rules, square corners, no shadows. Line-art figures print onto the stock (multiply); cut-out renders keep their own colors on it.

STORY: The visitor learns he designs, machines and tests hardware; sees the capstone mechanism, its measured result and award; scans three more case studies by their images and four short entries; then emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name large and wide top left, nav top right (Work, Experience, Email, LinkedIn). Role and positioning line under the name, two lines max. Below a full-width ink rule: capstone title, then the exploded view at about 70% width with the numbered parts legend on the right; hover or focus a part rings it in stamp blue. Award stamp and result line visible at 1440x900 without scrolling.

FORM: Traveler (grounded list #3, assigned) fused with the section-drawing challenger's layout (competitive). Raises: iteration strips in case studies (darkroom test strips); two inks only (risograph); one plain line per project (WPA poster). Seed key 5b1f3ac6.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature motion
Status stamps land (quick press) as their project scrolls into view, CSS scroll-driven, off under reduced motion and invisible-free where unsupported.
